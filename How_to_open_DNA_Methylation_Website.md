**How to open DNA Methylation website**



Open File Explorer and go to:

C:\\1) Arbeit\\1\_Science\\Raleigh\_Lab\\Data\\Methylation\_Website\\MeninMethylClass\_V3\\



Double-click run\_app.bat.

&#x09;A black console window opens and stays open — that's the app running. Leave it open.

&#x09;Wait \~30–60 seconds the first time (it loads the classifier models). It's ready once the log stops scrolling.



Open your browser and go to:

http://localhost:7771



To stop the app later: close that black console window (or press Ctrl+C in it).



**From a terminal**



Press Win + R, type cmd, and press Enter to open Command Prompt.



Paste this line (keep the quotes) and press Enter:

"C:\\Program Files\\R\\R-4.5.2\\bin\\Rscript.exe" "C:\\1) Arbeit\\1\_Science\\Raleigh\_Lab\\Data\\Methylation\_Website\\MeninMethylClass\_V3\\launch.R"



Wait until it prints Listening on http://127.0.0.1:7771.



Open your browser and go to http://localhost:7771.



To stop the app: click the Command Prompt window and press Ctrl+C (or just close the window). Leave the window open while you're using the app.
